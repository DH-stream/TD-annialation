# Greenward art direction

Target: warm, chunky, heroic fantasy closer to World of Warcraft than Minecraft or toy-like modular asset packs.

- Organic silhouettes and layered armor/cloth shapes.
- Warm stone, wood, moss, and brass with saturated arcane accents.
- Hand-painted-style value separation through disciplined materials and lighting.
- Readable gameplay path and tower silhouettes from the normal strategic camera.
- Low-poly geometry is acceptable; literal cube construction and flat proxy planes are not.
